Realism64 / SM64 Coop DX for Wii U

This is a test build for Aroma. Console testing is limited, so it might not work.

1. Unzip it onto your SD card and merge the wiiu folders.
2. Put your own Super Mario 64 US ROM at:
   wiiu/apps/sm64coopdx/roms/baserom.us.z64
3. Put the card back and start the game from the Wii U menu.

No ROM is included. Mod sounds, online play and voice chat haven't been tested on a console. For Realism64 voice chat, turn on "Microphone on" in Options > Sound to use the GamePad mic.

If the picture is wrong, please send wiiu/apps/sm64coopdx/gfx-report.txt. It is written after about 5 seconds and again after about a minute.
